How shared safaris work

Pick a date

Choose your park, session and day. Every open day shows how many seats are left.

Choose your seats

Tap the seats you want. They are locked for you for 15 minutes while you finish.

Pay securely

Pay by card on a secure page, or reserve now and pay when the jeep fills up.

Get your e-ticket

Once 4 seats are paid the safari is on and your QR ticket is emailed. Not enough people? Full refund.

Good to know

When is the shared jeep confirmed?

As soon as 4 seats are paid. Everyone on the jeep gets their QR e-ticket by email at that moment.

What if not enough people book?

If a jeep doesn't reach 4 paid seats before the deadline, it is cancelled and every payment is refunded in full.

Can I cancel?

Where do you pick me up?

Free pickup from your hotel inside the pickup ring of your park (you drop a pin on the map when you book). Outside the ring, you meet us at our office.
