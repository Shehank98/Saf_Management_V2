    FAQ

    Good to know

    When is the shared jeep confirmed?

    As soon as 4 seats are paid. Everyone on that jeep gets their QR e-ticket by email at that moment, and other guests can still join the free seats.

    What if not enough people book?

    If a jeep doesn't reach 4 paid seats before the deadline, it is cancelled and every payment is refunded in full.

    Can I reserve now and pay later?

    Yes, where it is offered. When 4 seats are reserved we email everyone a payment link, and you have 24 hours to pay before the seat is released.

    Where do you pick me up?

    From your hotel, free, inside the pickup area of your park (you drop a pin on the map when you book). On the day you can see when your driver is on the way. Outside the area, you meet us at our office.

    Can I cancel?

    Are park entry tickets included?

    Park entry fees depend on the day and on your group. Message us and we'll confirm exactly what your price includes.

    What should I bring?

    A hat, sunscreen, water, a light jacket for early mornings, your camera and your e-ticket. Neutral colours are best for wildlife watching.
